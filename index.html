Chapter I · Heritage

The roots we carry.

Exhibit 01 · Bugis-Makassar

Rafli's Roots

This series returns to Rafli's Bugis-Makassar roots. The attire, textiles, and gestures carry a heritage passed through family, presented here not as something distant, but as a living part of who he is today.

Exhibit 02 · Jawa

From Her Mother

Dita's Javanese roots come from her mother's side of the family. This collection honours that inheritance through the quiet elegance of Javanese dress, its details, and the sense of grace carried across generations.

Exhibit 03 · Palembang

From Her Father

Through her father's side, Dita carries Palembang roots. Rich gold, structured silhouettes, and ceremonial detail become a portrait of that lineage and a tribute to the heritage at the heart of her family.

Chapter II · Interpretation

Tradition, seen through our eyes.

Exhibit 04 · Traditional Contemporary I

Woven Together

Bugis and Palembang textiles come from different traditions, each with its own patterns, techniques, and character. Yet both are bold in their use of colour, turning fabric into something expressive and unmistakably their own.

The fabrics do not need to look the same to belong together. Their differences sit side by side, complement one another, and create something even richer. In many ways, that also feels familiar to us.

Photographed by @byjatidiriono

View collection
Exhibit 05 · Traditional Contemporary II

Projection of Our Roots

Lontara and Javanese script appear as projections of the cultural roots we carry. Beyond representing where we come from, they reflect our appreciation for traditional scripts, from their visual beauty to the way they preserve language, memory, and identity.

Cast through light, they feel less like something belonging only to the past, and more like something that continues to live with us today.

Photographed by @byjatidiriono

View collection
Exhibit 06 · Peranakan

A Shared Appreciation

We have always been drawn to the vibrant colours, intricate kebaya, decorative details, and food of Peranakan culture. For this series, we created our own interpretation of that visual richness.

The colour palette also takes inspiration from Taylor Swift's Lover, one of our favourite albums. Our families first met over a meal at a Peranakan restaurant, giving this theme a small personal connection of its own.

Photographed by @azharbaizan

View collection

Chapter III · Us

Where we began, and where we played.

Exhibit 07 · Bappenas, Menteng

Where It Began

We first knew each other as coworkers at Bappenas. Back then, Menteng was simply part of our everyday routine: the office, the streets around it, and the small conversations in between.

These photographs return us to a time when we were still getting to know each other, without knowing that this ordinary part of our lives would eventually become the beginning of us.

Photographed by @fikriipra

View collection
Exhibit 08 · Mid-Century

Out of Character

For this series, we tried something people would not normally expect from us. The mid-century styling, colours, and setting gave us a chance to step outside our usual character and play with a completely different idea.

Not every image had to explain who we already are. Some could simply be about curiosity, experimentation, and seeing ourselves in a new way.

Photographed by @azharbaizan
